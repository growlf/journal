# Proxmox Test System
An IBM Tower form factor business system with 16 core dual Xeon, 32GB RAM, multiple 2.5" spindle drives amounting to a 2TB ZFS storage set. Remote management port, SPF+ ports and multiple 1G ethernet ports. It has a rather nice hardware RAID card that I do not actually use - ZFS fits my needs better.
