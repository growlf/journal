# Ollama Server
Frank is an unassuming behemoth, cobbled together from a mishmash of parts with all the finesse of a mad scientist's creation - Frankenstein's monster comes to mind, but instead of bolts in his neck, he has duct-tape and zip ties holding him together. His haphazard assembly belies the cutting-edge technology that lies within: high-end NVidia hardware that grants him lightning-fast access to the vast expanse of Ollama LLMs.

This unlikely machine is a vital lifeline for myself and others who rely on its capabilities. Its raw processing power allows us to tap into the collective knowledge and creativity of the Ollama network, unlocking new possibilities and insights with each passing day. Despite his rough exterior, Frank is an unsung hero, working tirelessly behind the scenes to facilitate innovation and collaboration in our community.️
