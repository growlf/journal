[Vaultwarden](https://www.vaultwarden.net/)

...I really don't care for large companies (aka "targets") housing my personal and home-lab info.  Vaultwarden is an open-source implementation that is completely compatible with renowned Bitwarden password manager. This self-hosted service gives me a solution that keeps my data safely encrypted and in my own control with minimal hassle or maintenance, while still offering commercial grade application support via Bitwarden's quality mobile, web, and PC clients.
