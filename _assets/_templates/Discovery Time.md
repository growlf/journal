## Purpose of the Change
Summarize why the change is being proposed. Clearly state the issue or opportunity it addresses and the expected benefit.

> [!Note] Example Phrasing
> *Edit the following text with your specific scenario*
> 
> The purpose of this change is to address \[issue/need\] by implementing \[general solution\]. This initiative seeks to improve \[efficiency/security/reliability/etc\] within the IT environment and align with organizational goals of relevant objectives
## Testing & Validation
All changes must be tested in a **staging environment** before production deployment, unless deemed unnecessary (standard or emergency changes). Document the testing process, results, and any issues encountered. Confirm whether testing shows the change will work as intended.

> [!Question] Example Phrasing
> *Edit the following text with your specific scenario*
> 
> Testing was conducted in a staging environment to verify that \[general-solution\] functions as intended. Observations included \[high−level result\], and any discrepancies or issues were documented for review.
## Expectations & Limitations
Define what the change is expected to accomplish, along with any known constraints. Document success criteria, performance considerations, and risks that may limit effectiveness.

> [!Danger] Example Phrasing
> *Edit the following text with your specific scenario*
> 
>The change is expected to \[intended outcome\], though limitations may include \[known constraints/risks\]. Success will be measured by \[criteria or metrics\].
## Dependencies & Migrations
Check for related systems, services, or processes that may be affected. Record dependencies and whether migrations to other solutions, platforms, or integrations are required.

>[!Warning] Example Phrasing  
> *Edit the following text with your specific scenario*
> 
> This change may affect \[related systems/services/processes\] and requires consideration of \[dependencies or integrations\]. Any necessary migrations or adjustments to other solutions have been identified and documented.
