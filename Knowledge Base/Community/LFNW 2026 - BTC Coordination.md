# LFNW 2026: BTC Expo-hall Coordination

> [!NOTE] The Instructor's Perspective
> We are two weeks out from LinuxFest Northwest 2026. The Expo-hall at BTC is the heart of the event. If we don't have the "ground game" sorted, we're going to have a lot of unhappy sponsors and a very chaotic Friday evening.

## Objective
To ensure 100% readiness for sponsor setup on Friday, April 24th, 2026, and to finalize the layout for the BTC Expo-hall.

---

## The PACE Plan for Hall Access

### **Primary (P): Friday Afternoon "Vanguard" Setup**
- **Action:** Students and the "Service Corp" crew arrive at 1:00 PM on Friday to begin floor marking and table placement.
- **Goal:** Hall ready for sponsors by 4:00 PM.
- **POC:** Garth Johnson & BTC Student Leads.

### **Alternate (A): Thursday Evening Prep**
- **Action:** If the hall is cleared by BTC staff early, perform basic floor marking on Thursday evening (5:00 PM - 7:00 PM).
- **Pros:** Reduces Friday stress.
- **Cons:** Security needs to be notified of late access.

### **Contingency (C): Saturday Morning Surge**
- **Action:** If Friday setup fails or is delayed, a 6:00 AM crew handles the remaining tables before the 9:00 AM opening.
- **Cons:** High stress, potential for sponsor delays.

### **Emergency (E): Tactical Yeti Intervention**
- **Action:** Garth and the core LFNW board handle the layout manually if the volunteer crew is unavailable.

---

## Expo-hall Layout Plan
The layout needs to balance traffic flow, power access, and accessibility.

### **Key Zones**
1. **The Core:** Major sponsors (Intel, System76, etc.) with high power requirements.
2. **The Community Row:** Local non-profits (Cascade STEAM, Bellingham Makerspace, BLUG).
3. **The Forge:** Demo area for hardware (E-Ink tags, homelab gear).
4. **The Nest:** Volunteer check-in and attendee registration.

### **Tasks for Students**
- [ ] Finalize the table count (Target: 45 tables).
- [ ] Map power drops to the floor plan.
- [ ] Coordinate with BTC Facilities for "Friday After 4:00 PM" security clearance.
- [ ] Create signage for sponsor zones.

---

## After Action Review (AAR) - Pre-Setup Phase
- **What is working?** Student engagement is high; we have 12 volunteers confirmed.
- **What is a "Manageable Mess"?** The current floor plan draft is on a physical whiteboard; it needs to be digitized into an Excalidraw or SVG for the vault.
- **The Teachable Moment:** Coordination isn't just about moving tables; it's about communication protocols. If the "Primary" plan for access fails, the students need to know exactly who to call (BTC Security vs. Garth).
