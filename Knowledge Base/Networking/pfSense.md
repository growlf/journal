# pfSense

> In the Army, we had "traffic control points." pfSense is your primary tool for directing data between different networks and securing your perimeter.

## Description
pfSense is a free and open-source firewall and router software distribution based on FreeBSD. It is one of the most popular choices for home lab enthusiasts and professionals alike.

## Knowledge Check
- [ ] Have you configured your WAN and LAN interfaces correctly?
- [ ] Are you using any packages like Snort or Suricata for IDS/IPS?

## Common Pitfalls
- **Hardware Compatibility**: Ensure your NICs are supported (Intel is generally preferred).
- **Complexity**: It has a steep learning curve, so start simple.
