### Plugins I Like
- [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw-obsidian.online/Welcome) - A picture is worh a thousand words they say - this is that tool for explaining data, networks, process flow, etc.
- [DataView](https://blacksmithgu.github.io/obsidian-dataview/) - Very useful in my local-only pages. Create SQL access to my content to generate concise logical views of content and other data sources.
- [Git](https://publish.obsidian.md/git-doc/Start+here) - Slick as heck, part of my publishing cycle for these pages.
- [Tasks](https://publish.obsidian.md/tasks/Introduction) - indispensable. Life changing, in fact. Local only.
- Power Mode - just fun and silly, but makes my happy to write more!
- Ollama - if you have the hardware for it, it is amazingly useful once you create some custom LLMs for your own use.
